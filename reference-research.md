# Reference research and design influences

ForgeWise AI is an independently developed student prototype. The following public repositories were reviewed for workflow and architecture ideas. Their code has not been copied into this repository.

- SupplyPilot: https://github.com/venuayyappa/SupplyPilot
  Relevant ideas: RFQ lifecycle, quote comparison, CSV/PDF quote intake, AI procurement assistant, human review before supplier email.
- ProcurePilot: https://github.com/phaneendrakatakam/ProcurePilot
  Relevant ideas: role-aware approvals, procurement lifecycle, auditability, separation between draft and authorized purchase actions.
- VendorBridge: https://github.com/Ghaithehasan/VendorBridge
  Relevant ideas: manufacturing procurement context, purchase requests, RFQs, vendor quotation versions and approval trails.
- RAASA: https://github.com/Danoosh88/RAASA
  Relevant ideas: Streamlit RFQ workflow, SQLite persistence and separation of business logic from UI.
- OpenStock: https://github.com/TMBeaver/openstock
  Relevant ideas: inventory, BOM, production costing and quotation workflow.

These projects are references, not endorsements or proof that ForgeWise has equivalent features. Check each upstream repository's current license and terms before copying or adapting any code. This repository's implementation and feature claims are documented separately.
