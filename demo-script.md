# 3-minute demo script

1. Introduce the challenge: small factories can lose margin when raw material and production costs are not considered together.
2. Enter a customer RFQ and show the clarification status.
3. Compare the supplier quotes by landed cost, not just headline price.
4. Show inventory coverage and how much material must be purchased.
5. Adjust production cost assumptions and show the total estimated cost.
6. Enter a customer expected price. ProfitGuard flags high, medium or low risk and computes a target-margin price.
7. Generate a quotation draft, then demonstrate the owner approval checkbox.
8. Close by showing the estimated-vs-actual cost variance loop.

Say clearly: the data is synthetic, the five agents are implemented as transparent Python modules in this MVP, and integrations such as ERP, WhatsApp and OCR are future work.
